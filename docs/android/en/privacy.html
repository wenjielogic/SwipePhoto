Privacy policy

SwipePhoto for AndroidHelp and recoveryPrivacy policy

SwipePhoto 0.5.0 (version code 6) is a test release for Android 11 or later. Sort photos locally in English, Simplified Chinese or Spanish. No account, advertising or in-app purchases.

Photo processing

Photo browsing, sorting decisions, favorites, queues and encryption run on your device. SwipePhoto does not upload photos or app usage to the developer. This Android build has no INTERNET permission and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Permissions

Photo access is used to display and organize the photos you authorize. Original-media metadata permission preserves the original file during verified private transfer; it is not location tracking. System authentication protects access to the in-app vault; the developer does not receive your fingerprint or screen-lock credential.

Storage and retention

Local decisions and internal vault copies remain until removed or app data is cleared/uninstalled. Verified .swpv recovery files are stored separately in Download/私密相册. These files use the fixed weak password 123456 and require manual recovery after reinstalling. Removing a public original is a separate Android confirmation; other copies may remain.

Web pages and support

Opening policy, help or download links uses an external browser and an internet connection. GitHub Pages, GitHub, Lanzou and your chosen cloud/file providers process requests under their own policies. This site adds no analytics, tracking scripts or forms. If you email support or post an issue, the information you choose to send is received through those services and used to respond; do not send sensitive photo content.
