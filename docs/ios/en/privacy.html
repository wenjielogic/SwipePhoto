Who this policy covers

SwipePhoto for iPhone is created by Wenjie Lu. This policy covers the iPhone app, these support pages and messages you choose to send to the support address below. The Android app has a different private-storage format; these iPhone instructions do not describe Android storage.

What the app handles on your device

The app has no account system, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. It does not send your photos, favorites, sorting history, private-album contents or backup passwords to the developer.

Photo-library permission lets the app display and organize authorized photos, request confirmed system hiding or deletion, and save photos restored from older encrypted backups. The app processes the photo data and related metadata needed for these features, such as photo identifiers and dates. Language preferences, sorting decisions, pending-photo identifiers and processing results are stored locally.

New private actions: Apple Photos Hidden

Swiping down only adds a local queue entry; the original remains visible in Photos until you confirm hiding. After confirmation, iOS changes the original’s hidden status without copying or deleting the photo. The original stays in Apple Photos.

Viewing Hidden follows the device’s iOS authentication settings. SwipePhoto does not directly open or unlock that album, and cannot list its contents while system protection is enabled. Deleting SwipePhoto does not delete or unhide those system-hidden photos.

Legacy encrypted storage and backups

The older in-app encrypted album remains for recovery using device authentication. Its files and index are encrypted and excluded from the app’s automatic backup. Its encryption key is protected through the iOS Keychain. This storage is separate from the system Hidden album.

You can export an encrypted .swpios backup to a destination you choose and import it using its export password. External backup files are controlled by you and that storage provider. Keep a verified copy outside the app and its password; deleting the app does not automatically decrypt or restore legacy photos.

Your choices, retention and deletion

Change or withdraw photo access in iOS Settings. Use Undo for recent local decisions while available, remove an item from the pending-private queue, or revisit kept photos from Settings. These actions do not delete the original photo. Completed system hiding is reversed in Apple Photos, not by the app’s Undo.

Local decisions and processing records remain until changed through the app or removed with its app data. Legacy private copies remain until you restore and verify them and remove the private copy, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app-container data, including any legacy copies still inside it; offloading the app is different and can retain its data.

Keychain items are managed separately by iOS and may remain after app deletion; they cannot recreate encrypted photo files that were deleted. Independent exports and originals in Photos remain subject to your actions and the chosen service. Manage them in Files, Apple Photos or the storage service you selected.

iCloud and services you choose

iOS may download originals stored only in iCloud Photos. When enabled, iCloud Photos can synchronize hidden status and deletion across devices. Exported backups may be stored or synchronized by a file provider you choose. These are user-directed system services, not a photo upload to the developer.

Apple’s diagnostics, App Store and iCloud services follow Apple’s settings and policies. Your selected file and email providers process data under their own terms.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to reply, investigate the issue and maintain the support conversation, not for advertising or mailing lists. Email providers handle delivery and storage.

Send only what is needed for the issue. Do not send private photos, encrypted backups, passwords or verification codes. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up, subject to legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support data; provider backups or legal requirements can limit immediate removal. We do not have access to your on-device photo library to delete or recover it for you.

This website and GitHub Pages

These static pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded third-party media or site-managed cookies. Visiting this website still sends network information to its hosting service. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in to GitHub.

Hosting and related service data are handled under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection. Following an external link opens a service with its own privacy practices. The app’s no-photo-upload behavior does not mean a website visit creates no network logs.
