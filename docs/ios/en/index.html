A little swipe.
A clearer photo library.

Keep the moments you love. Review your photos one at a time, collect decisions in a queue, and confirm the changes when you are ready.

01

No account needed

No sign-in, advertising or analytics SDK in the app. Photo permissions are managed by iOS.

02

Review before you change

Pending decisions survive an app restart. Confirm a batch when you are ready.

03

Hidden by Apple Photos

New private actions use the system Hidden album. The originals are not copied or deleted.

←

Left · queue for deletion

The original stays in Photos until you confirm the deletion batch.

→

Right · keep

Keep the photo unchanged and continue to the next one.

↑

Up · favorite

Add an in-app favorite. This does not change the favorite flag in Apple Photos.

↓

Down · queue to hide

Add to the private queue. The photo is still visible in Photos until you confirm hiding.
