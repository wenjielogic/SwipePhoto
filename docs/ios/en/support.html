Get started

SwipePhoto supports iPhone with iOS 17 or later. There is no app account or sign-in. Grant access to the photos you want to organize; limited access shows only the photos you choose. You can change access in iOS Settings.

Open Settings → Language to choose English, 简体中文 or Español. iOS permission dialogs follow your device settings.

Four directions, four decisions

←

Left · queue for deletion

The original stays in Photos until you confirm the deletion batch.

→

Right · keep

Keep the photo unchanged and continue to the next one.

↑

Up · favorite

Add an in-app favorite. This does not change the favorite flag in Apple Photos.

↓

Down · queue to hide

Add to the private queue. The photo is still visible in Photos until you confirm hiding.

Queues, results and Undo

Down swipes add to the private queue and its number badge without opening the old encrypted album. The queue and its processing records are saved locally and remain after closing and reopening the app.

Go to Private → Add photos, review the list, then confirm the batch. Each request hides up to 100 available, editable photos. Successful items leave the queue; failed or unconfirmed items remain for checking, retrying or removal. Removing a queue entry does not delete its original.

Undo reverses recent local sorting decisions while their history is available. Once a system hide or deletion request is submitted, Undo is not a way to reverse that system operation. Use Apple Photos to unhide a photo or recover an item from Recently Deleted, where available. The app does not empty Recently Deleted.

Find the system Hidden album

The app provides a viewing guide at Private → Add photos → How to view system-hidden photos. It does not directly open or unlock the system Hidden album. When system protection is enabled, the app cannot list its contents.

  1. Open Photos on your iPhone.
  2. On iOS 18, swipe up on the Photos home screen to find Utilities.
  3. Tap Hidden, then View Album.
  4. Unlock using the system’s Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode prompt.

Other iOS versions may arrange these controls differently. If Hidden is not visible, check the Photos settings for the Hidden album display option. To make a hidden photo visible again, choose Unhide in Apple Photos. If iCloud Photos is enabled, hiding and deletion can sync to your other devices.

Older encrypted photos and uninstalling

New private actions use Apple Photos Hidden. These photos stay in Apple Photos after you delete SwipePhoto; deleting the app does not unhide them.

The separate legacy encrypted-album entry remains for recovering older in-app photos and .swpios backups. Unlock it with device authentication, restore the photo to Photos and verify it there. A verified restored copy is required before the app lets you remove the old private copy.

Update directly; do not uninstall first. Before deleting the app, restore and verify old photos or keep a verified backup outside the app and its export password. Deleting the app can remove old in-app encrypted copies. They do not decrypt or restore automatically. Android .swpv backups and iPhone .swpios backups are different formats.

If something does not complete

Check photo permissions and whether the original is still available in Photos. A cancelled system confirmation does not count as success. Some items may be unavailable under limited access or no longer editable. Originals stored only in iCloud may need a connection.

If an item is marked unconfirmed, inspect Apple Photos before retrying. A queued photo is not hidden until the system request succeeds. Keep legacy backup files and their passwords; the developer cannot recover a lost backup password or deleted local photo data.

Send a support request

Include the app version and build, iOS version, phone model, steps and the message you see. Do not send private photos, encrypted backups, passwords or verification codes. There is no support upload form on this site.
